UUID Generator

A UUID (Universally Unique Identifier) is a 128-bit number used to uniquely identify information in computer systems. Generate UUIDs in various versions (v1, v4) with options to customize formatting and quantity. All generation happens locally in your browser for maximum privacy and security.

Version 4 (Random): Uses random numbers to generate UUIDs. Most commonly used when no specific requirements exist.

Version 1 (Time-based): Uses current time and MAC address to generate UUIDs. Useful when time ordering is important.
